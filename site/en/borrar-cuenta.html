Delete your account and data

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Rocket Rush does not require registration. An (anonymous) online account only exists if you have used friends or leaderboards. You can delete it like this:

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open Settings (gear icon on the title screen).
  2. Tap Privacy & help.
  3. Tap Delete my online account, then tap again to confirm.

Without the game (by email)

Email rocketrush.lostanarchygames@hotmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” and include your player code (RR-…) or your nickname and country. We will delete it within 30 days at most and confirm it to you.

What is deleted

  • Your public profile: nickname, avatar, country, language and stats (you are removed from leaderboards).
  • Your friend code, your friendships and pending requests.
  • Your Firebase account (anonymous or linked to Google) and your cloud progress backup.

We keep no copies of this data after deletion. Purchase receipts are kept by Google Play under its terms and the law.

Your game progress

Progress (levels, coins, items) is stored on your phone and, if you signed in with Google, also in the cloud (deleted with your account). You can erase it in Settings › Erase progress or by uninstalling the game.
