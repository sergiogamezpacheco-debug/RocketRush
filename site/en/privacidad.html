Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: you can play without giving us any data. Your progress is stored only on your phone. If you use the online features (friends and leaderboards), an anonymous account is created with your nickname and your stats. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are personalised only if you consent. We never sell your data. You can delete your online account from the game at any time.

1. Controller

Sergio Gámez Pacheco (Lost Anarchy Games), Spain. Email: rocketrush.lostanarchygames@hotmail.com.

Event settings and gift codes are downloaded without an account and contain no personal data. Daily-streak reminders are scheduled on your own phone. We do not ask for your real name, email or precise location, and we do not access your contacts, photos or microphone.

3. Payments

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details.

4. Ads and consent

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we show Google's consent form the first time. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & help › Ad privacy options. If you are under 16, no personalised ads are shown. If you buy “Remove ads” you will not see ads between levels; rewarded ads are always optional. More information: how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. The first time, we ask for your year of birth (stored only on the phone). Players who indicate they are under 13 get no online account, cannot use friends or leaderboards, and only see non-personalised ads suitable for all ages. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Who we share data with

Only with the providers we need to run the game, acting as processors or independent controllers:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.): anonymous account, database and website.
  • Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.): ads.
  • Google Play: downloads, payments and updates.

Google may process data outside the EEA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or disclose your data to anyone else, unless required by law.

7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time by emailing rocketrush.lostanarchygames@hotmail.com with your player code (shown in Settings › Privacy & help). You can change your nickname and avatar in the game and delete your online account yourself. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local authority.

8. Security

All communication is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules only allow each player to change their own profile.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the game.
